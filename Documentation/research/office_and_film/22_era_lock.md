## 2. The lock

| Rule | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Now | **1990** | the story year of the film and of Kane's tapes |
| Newest design date | **1993** | the film's "nothing post-early 1990s"; nothing designed later may appear (no Windows keys, Sept 1994; no USB, 1996; no ATX, 1995; no LCD monitors) |
| Oldest | **~1955** | second-hand stock is normal in a 1990 room: estate sales, hotel liquidators, worn office furniture |
| Printed dates | **≤ 1990** | any date on a label, binder, calendar or stamp |
| Labels | current / second-hand / timeless | current = the type peaked 1985–93; second-hand = peaked 1955–85; timeless = the form did not change |

## 5. Boundary dates checked for this note

- Windows key: Microsoft Natural Keyboard, September 1994 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Windows_key).
- ScotchBlue painter's tape: invented by 3M in 1988 (https://3m.com.au/3M/en_US/scotchblue/about-us).
- Halogen torchieres: in the US from 1983; CPSC and industry recalled about 40 million for repair in 1997 (https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/1997/CPSC-and-Industry-Announce-Corrective-Action-to-Improve-Safety-of-Halogen-Torchiere-Floor-Lamps).
- Steelcase Series 9000 panel system: 1973 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steelcase).
